# Google Business Profile

Everything to paste into the profile, written out so the wording matches the site rather than
being invented on the spot at the form.

Create it at [business.google.com](https://business.google.com). It has to be done from Ari's own
Google account, because Google verifies the business against a real person, usually by postcard or
phone. Nothing here can be filled in on your behalf.

## Why it is worth the hour

Three of the eight modules lean on it. Local SEO is largely this profile. Reputation means the
review link people are sent, which is a profile link. And "someone in the College Station-Bryan area
searches for what you do" is a map result before it is a web result.

## The fields

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Business name | AMORA Studios |
| Business type | **Service area business** — pick this, not a storefront. It hides the street address and shows the area served instead. |
| Primary category | Marketing agency |
| Additional categories | Internet marketing service, Website designer |
| Service area | College Station, Bryan, Brazos County |
| Website | https://amorastudios.net |
| Phone | the number enquiries should ring |
| Hours | set real ones, or mark it "by appointment" — an empty hours block reads as abandoned |

Do not add a street address unless you want it published. Choosing service area is the step that
keeps a home address off the map.

## Description

Google allows 750 characters. This is about 610, which leaves room to add to it later.

> AMORA Studios is a marketing studio in the College Station-Bryan area, working with small local
> businesses that are good at what they do but quiet online. We build the website, run the social
> accounts, make the content, and set up the things that turn attention into enquiries: local
> search, reviews, email follow up, and a monthly report that tells you plainly what
> happened. Everything is handled personally rather than handed to a queue, so you deal with the
> person doing the work. Start with one piece or the whole thing, and add to it when it makes
> sense. No long contracts and no jargon, just the parts of marketing a small business actually
> needs.

## Photos

The profile ranks better with images and looks unfinished without them. At minimum:

- **Logo** — `assets/img/logo-mark.svg`, exported to PNG at 720x720 on the dark background.
- **Cover** — `assets/img/og.png` is already 1200x630 and on brand.

## After it is live

- Add the review link to the site and to the follow up emails. That link is the Reputation module.
- Point the profile at `https://amorastudios.net` only once the domain actually resolves. A profile
  pointing at a dead domain is worse than one with no website at all.
- Google Business Profile has its own insights, but the site's own numbers live in PostHog. Traffic
  arriving from the profile shows up there under the Google referrer.
