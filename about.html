About

A small studio for small businesses.

AMORA Studios runs the marketing side of businesses that are too busy running the actual business. We build the website, handle the social, chase the leads, and answer the messages, so the owner can go back to the work they are good at.

Why we started

The good ones stay invisible.

Nearly every town has a business that is genuinely excellent and almost impossible to find online. The website was built once and never touched again. The social page stops in 2022. The phone goes to voicemail because someone is halfway up a ladder.

Meanwhile a competitor who is not half as good gets the call, because they turned up first in the search, replied in four minutes, and made it easy to book.

That gap is the whole reason AMORA Studios exists. None of it is complicated work. It is just work that nobody inside a small business has time to do consistently, and consistency is the part that actually moves the needle.

So we do it for them. Not as a one off project that gets handed over and forgotten, but as an ongoing thing that keeps running every month while they get on with their day.

What we believe

Four things we will not budge on.

№ 01

You choose, not us

We will tell you what we think would help most, and then we will build whatever you actually pick. Nobody gets sold a package they did not want.

№ 02

Plain english, always

No jargon in the reports, no acronyms in the meetings. If we cannot explain why something is worth paying for, it probably is not.

№ 03

Consistency over cleverness

A good post every week beats a brilliant campaign once a year. Most of our value is simply that things keep happening after the excitement wears off.

№ 04

It has to earn its place

Every service we run should bring in more than it costs you. If something is not working we say so and we change it, rather than quietly billing for it.

How we work

What the first two months look like.

The schedule

form B-01 · rev. 2026
Week one

We learn the business

A proper conversation about what you sell, who buys it, what a good month looks like, and what has already been tried. Then we agree the list of services and the monthly price.

Weeks two to four

We build the foundation

Website, brand, booking link, social accounts, email list, whichever of those you picked. This is the heaviest stretch of work and you will see most of it go live inside the first month.

Month two

It starts running on its own

Posts go out on schedule, follow ups fire automatically, enquiries come in and get answered. You approve the plan once a month and we handle the rest of it.

Every month after

We report and adjust

A short honest report on what happened, then we double down on whatever is working. Your dashboard is there the whole time in between, so a suggestion never has to wait for the report. Add a service, drop a service, or change direction whenever you want.

Who we work with

Independent businesses with real customers.

Trades, salons, clinics, cafes, studios, coaches, photographers, cleaners, and anyone else selling a service to people in their area, mostly around the College Station-Bryan area. Usually somewhere between one person and a small team.

What they have in common is not the industry. It is that the work is already good and the marketing is the bit falling behind.

We are honest about fit. If we do not think we can make you money, we will tell you before you sign anything rather than after.
