Questions

The things people usually ask.

Do I have to take all of it?

No, and most clients do not. Pick the two or three things that would help most right now. You can add more later once you have seen the difference.

How much does it cost?

It depends on what you choose and how much work each part takes. Most clients start from one of the three packages and adjust it, and anything on the services list can be added on its own. Your monthly price moves with what you have switched on. Send us your list and we will come back with a figure within one working day. There is no charge for the quote.

Am I locked into a long contract?

No. It runs month to month and you can stop with 30 days notice. Anything we built for you, like your website and your accounts, stays yours.

How long until I see something happen?

Social posting and follow up automations usually start inside two weeks. Websites take a few weeks depending on size. Search results are slower and we will tell you that honestly before you pay for it.

Do you work with businesses like mine?

We work with small local businesses and independent operators, so trades, salons, clinics, studios, cafes, coaches, and anyone selling a service to their local area. If you are not sure, ask.

Who owns the accounts and the work?

You do. We set things up in your name wherever possible, and if you ever leave you take the website, the accounts, and the customer list with you.
