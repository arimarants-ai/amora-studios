About

One dog, one groomer, one room.

Marlow & Bean is a single chair salon on the west side of Ann Arbor. It is deliberately small, because the thing that makes grooming hard on a dog is everything happening around them.

Owner and groomer

Priya Raghunathan

Owner and the only groomer at Marlow & Bean, handling every dog personally from the first look round to the last brush, which is the whole reason the salon takes one at a time.

How this started

Eleven years in a busy salon.

Priya groomed in high volume salons around Detroit for eleven years, eight dogs a day, most of them waiting in crates for their turn. The work was good. The conditions were not, and the dogs knew it.

Marlow & Bean opened in 2021 to test whether a salon could run on one dog at a time and still make sense as a business. It can, as long as the price is honest and the schedule is not overbooked, which is why we sometimes say we are full three weeks out rather than squeezing you in.

Marlow and Bean were the two dogs who made the case. One was terrified of dryers. The other would not go through a salon door at all. Both are fine now, and one of them is usually asleep by the window.

Where we are

West side of Ann Arbor.

Seven minutes from downtown, with parking directly outside, which matters more than it sounds when you are getting a nervous dog out of a car.

Open Tuesday to Saturday. Walk in nail trims Tuesday to Friday before noon.
