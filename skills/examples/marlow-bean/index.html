Why we work this way

Most dogs do not come home from the groomer happy.

They arrive to six other dogs barking, wait in a crate for two hours, get worked on by whoever is free, and come out clean and shaken. Owners notice. They put the next appointment off, the coat mats, and the groom after that is harder than it needed to be.

We built this salon around the opposite. One dog at a time means no barking room to wait in. The same groomer every visit means your dog is being handled by someone they recognise. And if your dog has had a bad experience somewhere else, tell us, because that changes how we start.

What we do

Five things, done properly.

Prices depend on size, coat and condition, so the page below explains what drives them rather than giving you a number that turns out to be wrong.

Full groom

A bath, a full haircut to the style you want, ears, nails and a blow dry that actually finishes.

Around two and a half hours for most dogs.

Bath and tidy

A wash and blow dry with the face, feet and sanitary areas tidied up. No full haircut.

Good between full grooms, or for short coats.

Puppy's first groom

A short, gentle session that teaches a puppy that the table, the clippers and the dryer are nothing to worry about.

For dogs under six months. Half price.

De-shedding

A bath with a de-shedding treatment and a thorough undercoat rake, for double coated breeds losing their winter.

Huskies, shepherds, retrievers, anything leaving hair on the sofa.

Nails and paws

Nail trim, pad shave and paw balm. Ten minutes, no appointment needed on weekday mornings.

Walk in Tuesday to Friday before noon.

How a first visit goes

Nothing happens to your dog on day one.

  1. 1

    Tell us about your dog

    Breed, coat, age, and anything that makes them nervous. A sentence is plenty.

  2. 2

    Come and look round

    Bring your dog in before you book anything. They sniff the place, we say hello, nobody gets on a table.

  3. 3

    The appointment

    One dog in the salon at a time. You get a message when we start and a photo when we finish.

  4. 4

    Going home

    We tell you honestly how it went, including if something was hard, so the next one goes better.
